# Bharat Guide visual design

A calm, independent reading and chat surface for people looking for official government information.

- Off-white background #F7F8F5, navy text #172B46, muted text #526174, borders #DCE2DF.
- Green #2C624D is reserved for source links and the book mark. Saffron #BC6E2D appears as a small header accent and keyboard focus outline.
- One family: Atkinson Hyperlegible, regular and bold. Main answers 18px, 17px on phones; comfortable 1.65 line height.
- Laptop and tablet (768px and wider): carousel under the headline and suggestions, with a smaller illustration on tablet. Bottom composer stays visible without scrolling.
- Phone (below 768px): carousel is not mounted. Existing Parliament illustration is a 51 KB WebP full-page background under an 88% light overlay. Answer cards and the input remain solid white for contrast. Question input stays fixed at the bottom. The independent-guide line stays visible.
- Cards have 16px corners and quiet borders. No government logos, national emblem, official seals or claims of official status.
- Carousel: four static generated illustrations; nine-second slides; pause, play and arrows; pauses during pointer or keyboard interaction. Reduced-motion preference disables autoplay.
- Conversation controls, answer rendering and backend behaviour are unchanged.

Illustration prompts and saved assets are recorded in design/illustrations.json. Generated artwork is illustrative, not official photography.
